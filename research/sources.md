Derived values (for Module 3, labelled as derived from the captions):
- 1941–1950: more than 3 km in 9 years, i.e. a lower bound of about 0.33 km/yr.
- 1941–2004: more than 7 km in 63 years, i.e. a lower bound of about 0.11 km/yr averaged.
These are lower bounds only, because the captions say "more than". Phase B must not
present them as exact rates.
