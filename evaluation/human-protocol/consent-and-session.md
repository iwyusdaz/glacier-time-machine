# Human playtest protocol (template, not yet used)

No sessions have been run. Use this template for future playtests.

## Consent (read aloud, record verbal or written consent)

- You are testing the software, not being tested. You can stop at any time.
- We will note what you do and say. No names are stored; you get a participant code (P01, P02...).
- The app stores answers only in this browser; we clear it after the session (Menu → My progress → Clear my data).
- For learners under 18: written consent from a parent or guardian, and assent from the learner.

## Session (45–60 min)

1. Pre-check in Module 1 (record the score).
2. Modules 1–5, think-aloud. Note: confusions, where they hesitate, any over-claiming about causes.
3. Post-check and transfer (record scores).
4. Debrief: "What can the photos not tell you?" "How sure are you about the cause, and why?"
