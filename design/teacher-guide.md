# Teacher guide

**Time:** 45–60 minutes (Modules 1–2 alone: about 10 minutes for a short or museum session).
**Works:** in any modern browser; text mode has every task if WebGL is unavailable or for screen-reader users.

## Flow

1. **River of ice (8 min).** Pre-check (not graded), 25 s flyover, how glaciers move. Ask: "If the ice keeps flowing downhill, how can the end move uphill?"
2. **Same place, different time (8 min).** Swipe 1941/2004 photos; tag changes. Two distractors check over-reading ("mountains are taller", "photos prove the cause").
3. **Measure it (15 min).** Time slider over the three dated photos; measure on the terrain; compute rates. Stress that "more than 3 km" makes 0.33 km/yr a **minimum**.
4. **What came next (8 min).** Predict, observe, explain; order the NPS succession stages.
5. **Why, and how sure? (15 min).** Sort claims vs evidence; write a confidence statement; self-check with the rubric; post-check; transfer to fictional Glacier X.

## Sensitive-topic approach

- Lead with observations, then causes. Retreat here began around 1750 (NPS), long before 1941; tidewater calving matters; NPS lists snowfall, topography and climate trends as factors.
- The evidence sort marks both one-cause extremes as too strong. Accept well-argued disagreement that uses evidence.
- Name the gaps: the course has no regional climate records, fjord depth or calving data.

## What the numbers are (and are not)

- Verified: 1941–1950 retreat >3 km and thinning >100 m; by 2004 >7 km northwest; Riggs up to 600 m retreat and >250 m thinning (USGS captions).
- The orange path is a ruler computed from the elevation model, not terminus positions. No dates between 1941, 1950 and 2004 are shown.
- One USGS file (`Field - 1950`) is dated 1941 on its own record; the app flags it and does not use it. Good discussion prompt: how would you resolve a date conflict?
- Succession: the order is from NPS; how long each stage takes at Muir Inlet is not stated.

## Data and privacy

Answers are stored only in this browser (Menu → My progress: view, download JSON, clear). Nothing is sent anywhere. The record is plain, unencrypted localStorage — clear it on shared machines with "Clear my data". Free-text answers are not graded automatically.
